    How it works

    Four stages, no black box.

    The same lifecycle applies to every service in the catalogue, so you always know what has happened and what comes next.

    1Received

    Your submission is logged in the registry and a tracking ID is issued to you and to your email address.

    2Validated

    The registry checks the attachments against the published requirements for the service you chose.

    3Under review

    The assigned unit performs the technical assessment. If anything is missing you are asked for it here.

    4Decision

    The outcome is published on your timeline, emailed to you and available as a printable record.

    Service catalogue

    Pick the service, and the portal handles the routing.

    Each service publishes the unit that owns it, the documents you need and the working-day target the agency holds itself to.

    Before you start

    Five minutes of preparation is the difference between a decision and a request for more information.

    • Use a monitored official email. Every notification, including the decision, goes to that address.
    • Name your files plainly. Organisation-Document-Year.pdf beats scan_final_v3.pdf.
    • Check the requirements list for your service on the form — missing attachments are the top cause of delay.
    • Keep the tracking ID. It is the only key to your record and to the printable receipt.
